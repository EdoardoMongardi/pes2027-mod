Collection of Information

Konami will obtain by legal and fair means Information specified by the Information Handling Policy.

Purpose of using Information

Konami will use Information collected by us for the purposes specified by the Information Handling Policy. If Information needs to be used for any purpose other than the specified purposes, Konami will, prior to use, revise the purposes of use specified in the Information Handling Policy.

Entrustment and provision of Information to third-party

As a rule, Konami will not provide Information to third parties, except where we obtain the consent of the person concerned; the Information Handling Policy provides for otherwise; the Information is transferred due to an organizational restructuring; Konami is required to disclose it under legislation; or disclosure is ordered by a judicial or administrative authority.

Konami will use Information specified by the Information Handling Policy for the purposes specified in the Information Handling Policy, jointly with the companies of the Konami Group (i.e., companies in which KONAMI GROUP CORPORATION has a direct or indirect stake that are, specifically, listed at [ https://www.konami.com/corporate/en/subsidiaries/]; the same applies hereinafter). In these cases, a company of the Konami Group that receives Information will use and manage it in accordance with this Policy. With respect to such joint use, KONAMI GROUP CORPORATION (its address and representative are found at https://www.konami.com/corporate/en/data/) will be responsible for the management.

Please note in advance that Konami may provide Information collected from customers to contractors to whom we outsource our business in order to achieve the purposes specified in the Information Handling Policy. Such contractor may include a third party providing services for advertising and publicity, data analysis, and so forth.

Konami may provide personal information to a third party in a foreign country in any of the above cases. For more information, please contact us using the contact information provided in the Information Handling Policy.

Request for disclosure etc. of personal information

If a customer wishes disclosure, correction, addition, removal, discontinuation of use, deletion, or any other disposition of personal information provided by the customer, the customer will submit an application through the procedure specified by the Information Handling Policy, and we will promptly respond to such request and application.

However, if the owner of such personal information cannot be identified, or because of other circumstances, we may be unable to respond to such request by a customer.

If a customer asks Konami to remove or discontinue using the personal information necessary for the provision of our services, Konami may no longer be able to provide the services.

Management of Information

For the handling of the Information of customers, our control division will implement appropriate management, including over a contractor. We will take safety precautions at a level that is appropriate and reasonable against leakage to the outside and risks of unauthorized access from the outside so that customers’ Information will be protected.

Revision of this Policy

This Policy may be subject to a revision. This Policy so revised will become effective from the date of publication on this website.
