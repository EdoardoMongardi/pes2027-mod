Usage of Android Advertising ID

Konami Digital Entertainment Co., Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "KDE") may use the Android Advertisement ID issued by Google LLC (hereinafter referred to as "Android Ad ID") in connection with the services KDE provides on Google Play.

The Android Ad ID means an identifier to identify your terminal accessing Google Play and it will be used (i) to analyze your behavior and/or (ii) to show you the advertising you may be interested in (hereinafter referred to as "Interest-based Ad”)(collectively, “Purpose”).

When KDE uses the Android Ad ID, KDE complies with the terms of use of the Android Ad ID set forth by Google LLC to the service providers and your "opt out” setting of the Interest-based Ad on Google Play requested by you. In addition, KDE uses the Android Ad ID for the Purpose only and does not associate the Android Ad ID with the personally-identifiable information or persistent device identifier (for example: SSAID, MAC address, IMEI, etc.,) without your explicit consent.
